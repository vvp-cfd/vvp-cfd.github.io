    Scientific visualization

    SciVis (scientific visualization) is the visual side of simulation results: velocity fields, streamlines, isosurfaces, particle trajectories and animations. This page collects short clips from LS-STAG and LS-STAG-3D simulations. Each clip is muted and loops; the poster frame is shown until you press play. Click a clip to play it.

    Method

    All clips come from the LS-STAG family. The solver uses a Cartesian cut-cell mesh and a level-set representation of the immersed boundary; moving bodies are handled without remeshing. The 3D cases use the 3D extension (LS-STAG-3D), while the 2D cases use the two-dimensional solver. Colours show the velocity field unless noted; the scales are qualitative and kept from the simulation output.
