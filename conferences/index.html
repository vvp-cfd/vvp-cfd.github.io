    Conferences and talks

    Many results related to LS-STAG, high-performance computing (HPC), numerical simulation software, and RISC-V have been presented at peer-reviewed conferences, in educational programs, and at industry events. First, a list of selected international conferences is provided. The full conference list is organized by year. The links lead to event pages, conference proceedings, presentations, or publicly accessible recordings (where available).
